**Architecture:** Versioned engine and frontend alongside preserved v3. Existing authenticated CAS service/storage stays authoritative. Shared public catalog contains no generated secrets. Practice shares the exact new engine through a generated bundle.

**Spec:** docs/SEVEN-DAY-SPEC.md

## Global constraints
7 days, 12 characters/6 places/4 tools/4 times, role/character randomized independently subject to case constraints; S unknown identity and neutral outcomes; no active secret projection; no resetting legacy rooms.

- Legacy engine/UI, account passwords and static asset packaging preserved.
